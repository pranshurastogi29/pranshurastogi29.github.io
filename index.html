    Paper
    • CLEF 2026
      Pranshu Rastogi @ FinMMEval 2026: Systems for Tasks 1 and 2. Prompt-Engineered Gemini for Multilingual and Cross-Lingual Financial Reports QA
      Outstanding Paper Award. FinMMEval Lab. Rastogi P.

      Financial question answering in English, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi, plus cross-lingual QA over financial reports and news, built entirely on Gemini 3.x with no fine-tuning. Per-question routing and language-specific few-shot prompts do the work. Self-consistency voting runs only on the 10–20% of questions a confidence gate flags as uncertain, and calculation questions use a two-pass extract-then-synthesise step anchored on exact table figures.

      PaperCode
    • ACL 2025 · BEA
      Phaedrus at BEA 2025 Shared Task: Assessment of Mathematical Tutoring Dialogues through Tutor Identity Classification and Actionability Evaluation
      1st in Track 5 (tutor identity), 5th in Track 4 (actionability). Tiwari R., Rastogi P.

      Tells expert and novice human tutors apart from seven different LLMs in math tutoring dialogues, using cross-response context augmentation and model ensembles (macro F1 0.970 on test). A second system judges whether tutor feedback is actionable, using selective attention masking and instruction-guided training.

      Paper
    • ACL 2025 · SemEval
      fact check AI at SemEval-2025 Task 7: Multilingual and Crosslingual Fact-checked Claim Retrieval
      5th cross-lingual, 10th monolingual globally. Rastogi P.

      Finds previously fact-checked claims that match a social-media post, across languages. Framed as learning-to-rank with fine-tuned bi-encoder sentence-similarity models, mostly under 500M parameters and trained on free Kaggle T4 GPUs. Reached 92% Success@10 monolingual and 80% cross-lingual.

      PaperCode
    • ACL 2025 · SDP
      Extracting Software Mentions and Relations using Transformers and LLM-Generated Synthetic Data at SOMD 2025
      2nd in the NER test phase (65% F1). Rastogi P., Tiwari R.

      Finds software mentions in scholarly papers, tags attributes such as versions and URLs, and classifies the relations between them. Fine-tuned DeBERTa models handle NER and pairwise relation classification, and LLM-generated synthetic sentences stretch a training set of about 1,100 annotated examples.

      PaperCode
